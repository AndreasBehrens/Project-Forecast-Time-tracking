Insight Arcs · Time Tracking | User Guide Insight Arcs – Time Tracking & Project Forecast · 1 User Guide – Time Tracking Employee Quick Guide Access link: https://timetracking.insightarcs.com Deutsche Version: ANLEITUNG.md This quick guide is aimed at employees. It explains the daily use of the "Time Tracking" menu item, which brings two areas together under one roof: • Project Time Tracking – project-related recording of work performed • General Working Hours – legally required attendance tracking incl. absences Note: Additional functions for project managers and administrators will follow in upcoming releases and will be added here. What is the solution designed for? Insight Arcs Time Tracking is a web-based solution that lets teams record their working hours in a way that is legally compliant and project-accurate. It combines two levels: • Project-based time tracking: How much time was spent on which project and which task – the basis for reporting, invoicing (billable / non-billable) and project forecasts. • General working hours (attendance): The legally required documentation of daily working time under the German Working Hours Act (ArbZG) – regardless of whether you are currently booking on a project. All data is stored GDPR-compliant in Germany and archived in an audit-proof, GoBD-compliant manner (10-year retention, tamper-evident verification chain). The interface is bilingual (German / English) – the chosen language is stored permanently per user. Login & Navigation 1. Open https://timetracking.insightarcs.com in your browser. 2. Log in with your credentials. 3. Click "Time Tracking" in the sidebar (desktop) or the bottom navigation bar (mobile). 4. Inside "Time Tracking", switch between the two areas using the tabs at the top: "Project Time" → book time on projects and tasks; "Working Hours" → attendance, absences, clock in–clock out. 5. The language (German/English) can be switched at the top right of the header – the setting is saved.
